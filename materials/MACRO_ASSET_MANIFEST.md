# Dedicated terrain control textures

## Current cliff runtime: ambientCG normal-only layers

The procedural cliff maps described below are now historical/unbound assets.
`cliff_ambient_normals.tres` supplies nine 2K BC5 OpenGL normal layers downloaded
from the requested ambientCG sources. No source albedo or roughness was added.
See `tools/AMBIENT_CLIFF_NORMALS_REPORT.md` and the JSON source manifest in
`assets/terrain/cliff_normals/ambientcg/` for current architecture and controls.

## Regional cliff landforms refinement

Existing geology/family maps are preserved. `cliff_landforms.res` and
`cliff_landform_slopes.res` add finite buttress and shelf fields with signed
derivatives, baked by `tools/build_cliff_landforms.gd`, seed 816394. Both are
512-square RGBAH with mipmaps (5.33 MiB combined). Regional world-space weights
now limit the prior vertical-rib treatment and allow quiet, ledged and buttressed
sections alongside it. See the newest section of the composition report for
controls, performance measurements and remaining visual limitations.

## Additive cliff-family composition

The sparse-structure assets below are retained. Three new shared 512-square,
mipmapped half-float maps add broad mass, slab and pitted/weathered contributions:
`cliff_families.res`, `cliff_family_slopes_a.res`, and
`cliff_family_slopes_b.res`. Their offline builder is
`tools/build_cliff_families.gd` (seed 327183). Combined payload is about 6.67 MiB.
World-space region weights blend these families with the retained sparse seams;
this is an additive composition, not another replacement mask. See
`tools/CLIFF_COMPOSITION_REPORT.md` for controls, debug modes and limitations.

## Current sparse-structure revision (supersedes eroded relief below)

Removed the absolute-value ridged field and its repeated 3.71-frequency slope
sample. The offline baker now places nine sparse broad primary recesses, three
short branches and four incomplete shelf breaks, with random origins, lengths,
widths and taper. No rectangular block primitives are used. Seed 891240.
The background is a very low-amplitude single-octave field, not cellular relief.
The baked map has 85.09% quiet area at the 0.08 recess threshold; generation
and validation assert at least 65%. This is map coverage, not a guarantee of
the same percentage after projections blend on a particular cliff.

R stores relief, G structural recesses, B broad weathering and A shelf breaks.
Signed derivatives remain in the shared RGH slope map. Formats, periods and
per-projection normal construction are unchanged. Two smaller-scale slope
lookups were removed. PBR normal strength is lower away from recesses. Runtime
cliff palette slot 8 now uses the already available Rock028 source, including
matching normal and roughness. No new downloads.

Earlier entries describe historical revisions, not current mask contents.

## Current revision: eroded relief (supersedes slab construction below)

The bevelled-rectangle generator and all three runtime fracture-network bindings
have been removed. Historical network assets remain on disk for recovery only.
`build_cliff_geology.gd` now bakes continuous ridged/eroded relief using seed
91732, frequency 0.0055, two octaves, and seamless edge blending. Channels remain
relief / recessed pockets / weathering / upward shelf exposure.

`cliff_geology_slopes.res` stores signed precomputed relief derivatives in RG.
Relief is 1024 RGBAH (10.67 MiB) and slopes are 1024 RGH (5.33 MiB), both
with mipmaps. Normals are constructed
separately for each world projection and then blended, not differentiated after
orientation blending. This avoids false gradients from changing projection
weights. World periods are 173x211 m and 229x263 m, with a supporting fixed
3.71-frequency slope sample. No camera-dependent UV scaling. No fracture-line
textures are sampled in normal rendering. Meso source Rock034 now covers 36 m,
with a restrained secondary projection sample and footprint-filtered normals.

## Cliff geological mass layer

`cliff_geology.res` is generated by `tools/build_cliff_geology.gd`, construction
seed 615829 and weather seed 91732. 1024x1024 RGBA half-float, full mip chain,
11,184,808 bytes of image data, shared across all chunks. R is bevelled mass
height, G sparse broad runoff, B weather/lithology and A interrupted shelves.
Half-float is deliberate: BC3/8-bit height derivatives produced visible grid
artifacts during testing. PNG is a channel preview only.

Two vertical world projections cover 185x220 m and 237x271 m, with a fixed offset.
68 randomly placed overlapping skewed slabs form broad faces without a regular
cell layout. The map is reusable, not guaranteed aperiodic. It supplies signed
surface relief and coherent coloration independently of the older joint maps.
No vertex displacement, parallax, runtime slab construction or per-chunk texture
generation is performed. Existing rock source families remain in use.

September 29 fracture update: `cliff_macro.res` is superseded at runtime by
`fracture_network_0.res`, `_1.res`, `_2.res`. Each is 2048px linear BC3 with mips;
R structural paths, G erosion, B ledges. Generated by
`tools/build_fracture_networks.gd`, seeds 87131/96304/105477, no third-party input.
See `tools/FRACTURE_NETWORK_REPORT.md`. The table below documents the earlier

## September 30 surface correction

Removed the product-of-sines patch field that produced the repeating brown
checker. Grass and meso maps were regenerated from baked irregular fields,
with seamless edge blending. They are still reusable periodic assets, not a
mathematical guarantee of non-repetition. Two fixed, differently scaled samples
reduce recognizable reuse. No expensive fracture generation runs per fragment.

Current grass projection is 920 m plus a rotated 0.613-frequency companion;
height bends coordinates by y/2400. Meso projection is 230 m plus a rotated
0.713-frequency companion; features within the texture occupy smaller scales.
Meso B now gates restrained low-slope bank sediment. Soil coverage remains
conditioned by exposure, drainage and slope, not solely by the texture.

All three fracture networks were regenerated with primary lengths 240–850
pixels and widths 8–18 pixels before tapering (2048px maps). Their existing
310x240 / 487x337 m projections are unchanged. A bounded surface-gradient
normal response now accompanies albedo/roughness recesses. No displacement
or geometry changes were introduced.

Generated locally by `tools/build_macro_masks.gd`, seed 41973. No external images,
website previews or new ambientCG downloads. Existing PBR palette remains under
its existing CC0 manifest. These new maps are project-generated procedural data.

All three runtime `.res` files: 2048x2048, BC3 RGBA, full mip chain, linear data
(no source_color hint), anisotropic mip filtering, repeat enabled. Approximately
5.33 MiB each including mips; shared by all terrain chunks. PNG previews are
development diagnostics, not shader inputs. Generation only runs as an explicit
development command, never in a worker or during game startup.

separate. Macro coordinates do not use camera position or distance. Two compatible
projections/periods reduce repetition but do not guarantee aperiodicity.
