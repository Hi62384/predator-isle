CONFIGURING AND USING THE PROJECT
Open main.tscn and select Terrain. Desert Preview makes a pure-Desert test world.
Desert Preview Feature selects one of the 13 reproducible landmarks. Empty or
Disabled retains the saved normal biome world. These do not rewrite presets.
Expand Settings > Desert Landscape > Desert, or open desert_settings.tres.
Tune regions/dunes/rock/basins/materials there; save and restart to apply.
Fracture Strength changes bedrock recesses and mapped rock relief. Wind Shelter
Strength controls sheltered deposition. Albedo Gain calibrates Desert brightness
without changing Grass or scene lights. Ripple/micro/macro strengths are separate.
Shift+F5 cycles Desert views; Ctrl+F5 returns to shaded. Dune debug colors:
orange transverse, cyan longitudinal, yellow barchan, purple star, green irregular.
Crest orientation is a field proxy, not exact local branching-ridge direction.
WASD/QE move, Shift boosts, wheel changes speed, F7/P shows/places a scale marker.
More usage detail: TERRAIN_TUNING.md.

LIMITATIONS OF THE APPROXIMATIONS
Shelter samples a cached uncarved bedrock envelope, not recursive full terrain.
Gullies/drainage are finite rooted approximations, not hydraulic simulation.
Star/parabolic forms are analytic approximations; mathematical existence does
not certify every viewpoint's recognizability. Bedding coordinates interpolate
over the actual mesh; no claim of exact sub-triangle stratigraphic surveying.
Oases use eligible sea-level depressions, not elevated independent lake planes.
No unsupported heightfield overhangs, foliage system or animal work was added.
