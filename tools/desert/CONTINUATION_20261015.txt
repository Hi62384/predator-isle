IMPLEMENTED IN THIS CONTINUATION (SOURCE MECHANISMS, NOT NATIVE ACCEPTANCE)
1. Rooted fine badlands tributaries. Each eligible catchment adds at most four
   short descriptor curves. A local branch ends at an existing tributary node;
   its child joins the actual curved parent at t=.55 with the matching graded
   floor. Fixed tile bounds cull candidates. Broad smooth support and head fades
   keep these heightfield cuts finite. Main channel floors still take precedence.
2. Within-segment channel width varies by up to 22%, with zero variation and
   zero variation derivative at endpoints. Trunk topology is unchanged.
3. Cached upstream shelter. Three evaluations of a common uncarved bedrock
   envelope (current point, 96 m upwind, 288 m upwind) run only at eligible cached
   256 m region nodes. Smooth interpolation drives lee sand supply, bounded
   deposit relief, exposure and diagnostics. There is no recursive sample() or
   per-vertex neighborhood terrain search.
4. Independent bedrock recesses before sedimentary remapping, smoothly gated
   by bedrock exposure and absent from lake floors/active channel beds. A
   separate existing Rock050 normal source supplies medium/broad rock relief.
5. Seven-layer Desert geology arrays reference the original six family assets
   plus independent existing Rock030 color/normal images. Coherent rock
   provinces blend across a broad boundary. The family selector still chooses
   at most three structural families; a rock transition can sample two rock
   sources. Complete older six-layer pairs remain fallback candidates.
6. Canonical pre-shelf bedding is transported through CUSTOM2, the exact-height
   cache, vertex samples and skirt copies. The material no longer tries to
   reconstruct Desert strata by dividing final mixed/coastal height. Derivatives
   are taken before material branches for footprint filtering. No extra Desert
   channels allocate on pure Grass chunks.
7. Four additional integrated debug views: dune-family weights, dominant crest
   orientation proxy, upstream shelter and canonical bedding. Existing debug
   indices remain unchanged. HUD reports upstream shelter and rooted gullies;
   its pale-deposit reading is explicitly named Mineral mask, not genuine salt.
8. Disabled Desert skips optional Desert palette/fracture binding. Inactive
   dunes clear family diagnostics. Pure sand and bypassed drainage reset
   published drainage details so stale prior queries cannot drive these fields.

PARTIAL / LIMITATIONS
- Gullies are a bounded analytic hierarchy connected to the existing catchments,
  not hydraulic erosion or a proof of globally monotone final-height flow.
- Shelter uses a regional uncarved bedrock envelope. It does not raycast every
  actual outcrop, dune or near-surface obstruction.
- Bedding matches the canonical per-vertex coordinate. Interpolation cannot
  recover shelves that coarse geometry does not resolve; coastal/mixed visual
  alignment and skirt appearance still need native inspection.
- Crest orientation view is the transported wind/longitudinal-family reference,
  not a measured crest tangent for every barchan horn or star arm.
- Parabolic/stabilized and star dunes remain mathematical constructions rather
  than physical sand transport/stabilization simulations.
- Canonical CPU detail is not removed by camera distance. Existing skirts and
  streaming partition behavior are retained, not replaced with new stitching.
- Added cost: two rock noise queries in eligible bedrock; bounded local gully
  candidates; cached upwind envelopes; 16 extra vertex bytes on Desert chunks;
  a seven-layer array and one shared fracture texture; a conditional second
  rock source in transition pixels. No speed or memory regression result exists.
- Historical richer-Desert CPU nonregression gate failed. This continuation does
  not supersede that result or claim a measured optimization.

Sandstone -> Wind Shelter Strength controls cached lee accumulation.

families (orange transverse, cyan longitudinal, yellow barchan, purple star,
green irregular), crest reference, upstream shelter and canonical bedding.

is NOT requalified after these geometry changes.
