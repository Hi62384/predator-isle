# 51. COMMON FAILURES TO AVOID

The previous Grass biome implementation went through many iterations because some changes replaced one procedural-looking pattern with another.

Do not repeat this mistake.

Specifically avoid:

**Smooth noise terrain**

The Desert must not just be Perlin noise with sand.

**Identical dunes**

Dune fields must have real structural variation.

**One orange texture**

Use distinct surface materials and coherent regional color palettes.

**Noise-painted rock**

Rock exposure must correspond to terrain geology.

**Overly complex everywhere**

Keep substantial calm areas.

**Repeated textures**

No obvious sand, rock, or material-mask tiling.

**Artificial cliff patterns**

No grill-line cracks, scratch decals, sponge textures across every wall, or repeated rock tiles.

**Uniform water density**

Desert water must be rare and geographically supported.

**Too much geometry**

Tiny ripples and rock grain belong in normal maps, not dense terrain meshes.

**Performance regressions**

Do not trade away chunk-generation speed just to increase invisible detail.
