DESERT FOLLOW-UP FIXES — 15 OCTOBER 2026

HISTORICAL RECEIPT: superseded by CLOSEOUT_20261015.txt (20 checks and expanded
three-seed/five-family rendered review). The measurements below describe the
previous delivery, not the latest source fingerprint.

Scope: address steep-face texture stretching/striping, blotchy sand-rock
transitions, overly smooth rock slopes, and generation bottlenecks. No exports,
new third-party assets, commits, pushes or history rewrites were performed.

RETAINED FIXES
- shaders/desert_material.gdshaderinc: actual triangle derivatives correct the
  projection axes where the canonical smooth normal disagrees with the rendered
  escarpment. This removes long stretched ribbons without changing Grass.
  Signed UV axes use that same corrected normal, including the case where a
  smooth crest normal has a zero component but its triangle projection does not.
  Multi-scale normal slopes have a bounded 48-degree tilt, avoiding black tangent
  lighting when the overlapped maps add together. Diagnostic Surface 3 reproduces
  the former projection for a controlled visual regression comparison.
- Material weights now normalize their continuous physical contributions rather
  than subtracting a ranked cutoff. In nearly tied mixtures, the old subtraction
  amplified tiny variations into isolated flat-colored islands. Exact-zero and
  sub-0.4% contributors still skip texture fetches. Typical surfaces use 2–4
  families; boundary pixels can use up to six, an explicit GPU quality tradeoff.
- Removed the extra curvature-derived sand coverage and reduced the 27 m rock
  border perturbation. Canonical sheltered deposition still controls aprons.
  Curvature/crest proxies no longer select albedo material families: compaction
  follows canonical drainage, basin and deposition. A transported dune-elevation
  proxy only modulates fine normal/ripple relief and restrained crest tint.
  This removes small stencil-sized islands and prevents pale patches appearing
  on sparse distant sand meshes, without flattening dunes or removing textures.
- Added a footprint-filtered 380 m rock-normal relief band, alongside 28/95 m
  detail, so distant faces retain readable geological variation.
- scripts/desert_settings.gd / desert_sampler.gd: Rock Relief Metres exposes
  the existing 95 m bedrock form field with a 24 m default amplitude before
  ruggedness/exposure weighting. Connected drainage beds remain protected.
- scripts/terrain_mesh_builder.gd: height-only derivative probes avoid material
  payload construction. A separate bounded 8192-entry cache cannot poison vertex
  metadata. Typed local values and unrolled scalar packing remove redundant
  per-vertex Variant indexing without changing byte quantization.
  Fast Normal Sampling uses two fixed-world 32 m forward probes in dry pure
  Desert, with a smooth Desert-dominant transition. Pure Grass/water retain the
  previous central derivative. Shared-coordinate normals remain canonical.
  This changes Desert lighting normals, NOT terrain height, geometry or material
  payloads. Its now-unused Desert curvature stencil fades out with that transition.
  The central mode remains available for art comparison in desert_settings.tres.
- scripts/desert_drainage.gd: bounded 128 m candidate subtiles conservatively
  skip irrelevant descriptors. Profiles, ordering and Newton solves are retained.
- scripts/chunk_terrain.gd: distant batches are bounded by estimated sampling
  work (4096 probes) as well as the existing user chunk-count cap. This reduces
  long-tail work imbalance without increasing worker count or changing meshes.
- tools/desert/preview_desert.gd: debug parameters now update ALL active LOD
  material duplicates. Previously only LOD0 changed, invalidating much of the
  medium/aerial diagnostic evidence. The final full sweep replaces those views.

FINAL NATIVE RESULTS / REVIEW
Godot 4.6.1, OpenGL Compatibility, RTX 3060 Laptop, 1280 x 720, four workers.
Final full-world fill: 2.892 s (previous delivered run: 3.383 s).
An otherwise identical central-normal reference filled in 3.262 s; the paired
two-probe run reduced that measurement by 11.35%. Against the previous delivery,
the measured reduction is 14.52%. These are single-run comparisons, NOT a
statistically established general speedup. No worker count or mesh density cuts.
Regional teleport settle: 2.591–3.462 s. Static frame p95: 6.280–6.387 ms;
GPU p95: 2.021–3.214 ms. Maximum partition swap: 10.001 ms; plan: 6.074 ms.
Coverage, visible-overlap, LOD-balance and cache checks: zero failures.
The refreshed simple baseline filled in 1.270 s. Therefore baseline CPU parity
is NOT reached (the richer biome is about 2.28x this refreshed reference).
The earlier 1.63x comparison used the previous baseline run and is superseded.

All 39 shaded and 33 diagnostic captures were refreshed after final geometry
changes, and the four contact sheets were inspected. Long stretched near-face
ribbons are corrected at the reviewed locations; continuous material mixing
removes the ranked-mask amplification and softens the transitions. Wider rock
relief is visible, although distant heightfield meshes still show faceting and
some smooth or patchy areas remain. Barchan crests and longitudinal ridges are
visible, but this is not blanket acceptance of every dune family/seed/viewpoint.
The legacy-projection comparison uses the same final geometry/camera, not a
different seed or lighting setup. Its captures have _legacy_projection suffixes.

These are completed bug fixes and measured optimizations, not an assertion that
all subjective art requirements or the original CPU nonregression target passed.

An experimental 8 m interpolated dune cache was tested and REMOVED: its small
microbenchmark benefit did not improve native loading. No approximate dune
reconstruction or Dune Field Step option is shipped. The original analytic
dunes, configured widths and saved-world compatibility remain intact.

USE
Select Terrain in main.tscn, then Settings > Desert Landscape > Desert. Rock
Relief Metres is in Sandstone; existing Fracture Strength, Wind Shelter Strength,
micro-normal, macro-detail and ripple controls remain independently editable.
Save and restart to regenerate. Desert Preview Feature selects a landmark.
Fast Normal Sampling is enabled by default; disable it to use the previous
central derivative lighting at additional generation cost. It never changes
the terrain's actual heights, PBR control payloads or saved biome geography.
The saved biome preset and Grass source material remain unchanged.

ACCEPTANCE BOUNDARY
Functional correctness and the specific reviewed artifact fixes are distinct
from photographic parity, every possible seed, or zero cost for richer geology.
The richer biome is still more CPU-expensive than the simpler reference. Native
benchmarks report that cost rather than presenting cache microbenchmarks as an
end-to-end speedup. No export or additional asset download is needed.
