TEXTURE-LED MATERIALS
Runtime arrays use four reviewed AmbientCG CC0-1.0 sets:
  Snow005 - clean powder, photogrammetry
  Snow006 - compacted crust, photogrammetry
  Snow014 - directional meso/wind-detail normal, photogrammetry
  Ice004  - cracked ice, an AmbientCG pre-baked procedural asset
Existing Rock035/Gravel020 maps remain for exposed stone/mineral ground.
There is NO runtime analytic fracture, cosine-ripple or synthetic snow-relief
surface generator. Procedural geography/accumulation and low-cost scan-sample
blending masks remain; they do not replace the sourced surface detail.
Glacier ice coordinates follow the published flow field; normals transform back
to world space before blending. Frozen-lake ice retains its own world repeat.
Independent powder/crust/ground/rock/ice strengths and scales remain available.
Sample-offset blending suppresses square repeats at medium range as well as near.

CONFIGURATION / VIEWING
Open snow_settings.tres in the Godot Inspector, or Terrain > Settings > Snow
Terrain > Snow. Restart the running scene after editing immutable worker settings.
Scale values are metres per repeat. Master Normal Strength = 0 disables every
Snow overlay. Macro Strength controls sourced wind-detail normals, not synthetic
ribs; old procedural ripple controls are removed.
Enable Terrain's Snow Preview and choose Snow Preview Feature. The saved world
preset remains Grass-only: a normal launch does not silently switch it to Snow.
Alt+F5 cycles Snow diagnostics; Ctrl+F5 restores shaded; F7/P toggle/place scale.
See TERRAIN_TUNING.md for details and offline rebuild commands.
Preview measurements were refreshed without moving the 17 tested coordinates.

LIGHTING DIAGNOSIS CORRECTION
The initial proposed Sun correction was based on a misreading of the scene's
serialized transform. A runtime basis check proved the original Sun already
emits downward: (0.289036, -0.788011, -0.543597). The temporary flip was restored.
Final main.tscn has no change. A dedicated lighting-direction regression test
now checks the actual runtime basis. Sky, Sun intensity/colour, shadows and ocean
shader remain unchanged. Final renders/tests use the original verified daylight.
