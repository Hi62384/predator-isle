Original procedural salt crust, authored for this project on 15 October 2026.
No downloaded image, third-party material graph, photograph or AI image is used.
This is NOT a photoscan and must not be described as scanned salt.

Recipe: tools/desert/bake_salt.gdshader; baker: tools/desert/bake_salt.gd.
Irregular periodic evaporite cells with raised crystalline seams, fine salt
grains and correlated roughness. Physical repeat: 2 metres. Baked offline.
Color: sRGB; roughness/height: linear; normals: OpenGL +Y.
Prepared resources: BC3 2048px color/roughness and BC5 1024px normal XY,
with linear-light albedo mips, linear alpha and renormalized normal mips.

The project's pre-existing ambientCG/Poly Haven sources are unchanged and keep
their recorded provenance. No new third-party salt asset was downloaded.
